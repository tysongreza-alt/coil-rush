# Wildkin 3.0.0: The Living Wilds

Release approved October 31, 2026.

## Adventure
Six linked regional chapters, 18 mapped field adventures and 216 authored locations. Each chapter connects three relays and a chosen story arc to a distinct Guardian encounter. Optional stories, hidden reports and regional requests lead to persistent choices and regional homecomings. A three-trial finale responds to regional resolutions and expedition knowledge. Playable guided practice and objective-based chapter navigation support new players.

## Collection and strategy
30 original creature families / 60 illustrated forms, evolutions, Aurora catch-up, three-companion tactical combat, signatures, passives, intentions, switching and charms. All owned companions heal after completed victories, captures, defeats and escapes. Sanctuary search, filters, favorites and four party presets. Research rewards, achievements and habitat interactions/cosmetics.

## Replay and platform
Six regional expedition circuits, 36 events, three difficulty tiers, supplies, preparation, extraction and checkpoint teams. Four arena brackets / 12 trainers with scouting. Guardian rematches and rare hunts. Six optional synthesized regional scores, mute, reduced motion, battle speed, local mobile icons and offline shell. Validated backups, migration, explicit reset, corruption recovery and transactional save-conflict protection. No ads, accounts or purchases.

## Verification and approved limitations
64 automated regressions, six clean main campaigns (all starters / both approaches), completionist traversal and 2,700 real-engine strategy fixtures. Browser checks cover all eight screens at narrow phone width, puzzles, onboarding, artwork and actual IndexedDB concurrency.

The owner explicitly approved publication of the reviewed candidate despite unverified campaign duration and unavailable family/physical-device testing. Campaign agents finish the expanded main route in 30–48 battles without defeats. This is not evidence of 8–12 hours; no duration guarantee is made. Physical iPhone Safari/Home Screen, target-age enjoyment, audible review and device battery/performance acceptance have not occurred. Quota and offline/update failure coverage includes models rather than exhaustive device testing. Approval permits release with these disclosed limitations; it does not turn unperformed checks into passed checks.

## Update and fresh start
Camp > Check updates > Install downloaded update. Finish an active encounter before installation. Existing saves remain compatible. For a clean adventure: Camp > Export backup, save the file, then Start a fresh adventure, check the explicit confirmation and confirm. Choose a starter. Never clear Safari website data as an update step.
