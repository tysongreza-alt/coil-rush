# Draft App Store Product Page: One More Move

**Not approved for use until commercial name, native build, and assets are finalized.** This file deliberately uses the browser game's familiar working title. No association with the publishers of other similarly named games is implied.

## Proposed listing

**Working name:** One More Move (requires naming clearance and likely change)  
**Subtitle:** Tiny puzzles. Clever routes.  
**Primary category:** Games, Puzzle  
**Secondary category:** Games, Casual (subject to App Store Connect)  
**Model:** Paid upfront, target US $0.99, no additional purchases or ads.

**Proposed promotional text:**
One little spark. Five distinct worlds. Eighty puzzles to solve at your own pace, then replay to find the perfect route.

**Proposed description:**

Move one square. Find the glow. Make every move count.

Guide a friendly spark through five charming puzzle worlds. Collect each golden glow and reach the doorway before you run out of moves. The catch? The board wraps around, and every new world adds a twist.

SIMPLE TO LEARN
Swipe or use the direction buttons to move your spark one square at a time. There is no timer. You have room to think, experiment and try again.

FIVE LITTLE WORLDS
- First Light: Discover clever routes around an open board.
- Stone Garden: Learn to navigate around solid stones.
- Frozen Rails: Explore locked paths and one-way routes.
- In the Right Order: Collect numbered glows in sequence.
- Moonshift: Use magical paired portals to reach your goal.

MORE TO DISCOVER
- 80 puzzles with three-star mastery
- Optional hints, undo and quick retry
- A daily challenge selected from the puzzle collection
- Three local player profiles
- Original, understated visual and sound design
- Play offline
- No advertising, accounts or subscriptions

A small world of big ideas. Just one more puzzle.

**Proposed keywords (revise after final title and research):**
offline,logic,brain,think,maze,relaxing,tile,casual,glow,stars,route,swipe,challenge,one finger

## App Review notes draft

“One More Move is a self-contained, offline, single-player puzzle game. All 80 levels are included in the paid app with no login, online services, in-app purchases, or advertisements. Tap Play, swipe or use on-screen arrows to move the spark, collect all glows, and reach the doorway. The board edges wrap. Levels can also be browsed directly. A new player can complete the first puzzle in two moves by moving right twice. No account or special reviewer credentials are required.”

Update this description to match the **final native binary**, not the browser test. Disclose any future functionality or purchases in review notes.

## Metadata checks before submission

- Name, subtitle and keywords must fit App Store Connect character limits.
- Do not label as “for kids” or market within Kids Category unless meeting its dedicated requirements.
- No claim that puzzles are hand-designed: content originated from seeded generation and was solver-verified.
- Daily challenge uses an existing puzzle rotated by local date. Do not imply newly released content every day.
- Age rating from Apple's current questionnaire, not assumed.
- App privacy disclosures and privacy-policy link from final executable, including any third-party SDKs.
- Working support page with real contact information from the owner.
- Real screenshots, accessible onboarding and physical-device stability required.
